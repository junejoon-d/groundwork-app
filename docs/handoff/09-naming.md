# Naming: replacing "Groundwork"

**Status: open.** Keep "Groundwork" as the working title in code and docs until Danny picks a name. Avoid hard-coding the brand where a config value would do (app name, bundle display name, store copy), since it will change.

## Why it's changing
Decided 2026-10-18. Six exact-name fitness apps already exist (GROUNDWRK, Groundwork: Breathe & Move, a Groundwork coach app, Groundwork Athletics, GroundWork Training in London and others), so the name can't win search.

## Danny's preferences
- Support first ("they've got me") plus belonging. Any activity, not only running.
- For a whole community, not pairs. Must work beyond DC.
- Feel: steady and grounded first, then bold, then warm.
- A real word, or a real word with a slight twist. One word if possible.
- No borrowed foreign words without a real tie (the Korean, Spanish and Japanese candidates from the original project were dropped).
- Wants a brand-origin story with each idea. No budget cap.

Danny likes **"Steady"** itself, but it has 65 live marks (including STEADY, LLC in Virginia for sports training, and lululemon's STEADY STATE) and every domain is taken; attorney consult advised before choosing it.

## Ruled out by Danny
Crewly, Foothold, Stalwart, Footing (likes the idea and the French link, not the word), Steadies, Steddy, Stedi. "Maybe" on Groundswell and Mainstay, but both screened risky (Groundswell MMA holds a class 41 fitness mark in Maryland; Choice Hotels holds MainStay).

## Ruled out by research (selection)
Platoon (Apple's mark), Muster, Cohort, Brigade, Corps, Pillar, Keepers, Mainstays (Walmart), Ironwood, Abide, Rocksteady, Hardy, Surefoot, Spotter, Belay, Cairn, Paceline, Orka, Parea, Props, Holdfast, Strut, Stride, Hitch, Regs, Tandem, plus a long list from the original project (Spar, Pair, Deuce, Pacer, Domestique and more).

## Lessons the search produced
- Top apps pair a short brand with a descriptor ("Strava: Run, Bike, Walk"). Plain English one-word names are mostly taken; bent words (Hevy, Gravl, Runna) are how common words become ownable.
- Community brands name the group or the connection (Peloton, Hinge, Bumble); support brands name the promise or what they do for you (Janji, New Balance, ASICS).
- Exact .com and .app are taken for every real word; launch on .run or joinX.com and buy the .com later.
- Checks so far are a screen, not clearance. An attorney runs a full search before filing.
